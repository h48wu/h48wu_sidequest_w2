## Project Title
Side Quest Week 2: Interactive Shape Switcher

---
## Description
> A procedurally generated vertical platformer that represents the feeling of anxiety. As the player climbs higher up the tower, the "anxiety level" increases, causing the screen to shake violently, the color palette to shift from calm blue to panic purple, and unsettling text to appear in the background. The player must collect chamomile tea to lower their anxiety levels and steady the camera while navigating to the portal at the top. The game features a dynamic camera system, fall damage logic, and randomized level generation.

---
## Interaction Instructions
> Start Game: Press SPACE on the main menu.

Movement: Press A to move Left, D to move Right.

Jump: Press SPACE to jump.

Restart: Press SPACE after a Game Over or Win screen.
